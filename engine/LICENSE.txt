# Engine components

This folder contains third-party binaries and neural networks required by YiYou.

## KataGo

- `katago-opencl.exe`, `katago-eigenavx2.exe` — KataGo engine v1.18.1
- `b10c128.bin.gz`, `b6c96.bin.gz` — standard networks
- `b10c384.bin.gz` — the human-simulation network (`b10c384h6nbttflrs`) used for
  human-like AI opponents
